History of Volkswagen Beetle!

In 1934, the Volkswagen brand started as a Nazi project.
Names of the brand:

  1. Käfer (1934)
  2. Gesellschaft zur Vorbereitung des Deutschen Volkswagens mbH (1937)
  3. Volkswagenwerk GmbH (1938)
Although it had started as a Nazi project, the brand moved away from its Nazi Roots. The car is known as Germany's economic miracle, with many exports. Volkswagen manufacutring allowed for the company to make many new products and help advance techonology in the car world.

Why People Love the Car?

Many people love the cars, both now and then. Not only does Volkswagen make the Beetle line, they have vans, sedans, and SUV's. allowing people to have many cars to choose from.
Beetles, in specific, are known as classic cars. They are also known as the "family" car. The colors they come in, the size of the car. the style, and the drive style are all things that attracte people to these cars. Overall, the cars are really cute, hence why people also love them. While the cars may be unreliable, with good maintence, the cars can last for years. The style of the car is just so iconic, there aren't many who don't know the car.
